I have lived and worked in different countries, which has given me a global outlook and a broad perspective. Experiencing different cultures and ways of working has made me more adaptable, creative, and curious, always ready to take on new challenges and solve problems in different ways.

I am fascinated by how we can assess 21st-century skills in a more dynamic and meaningful way, capturing real-world problem-solving through digital interactions and adapting to the fast-changing world. This curiosity has led me to explore different fields, ranging from computational linguistics to information retrieval and data-driven skill assessment. My research focuses on how machine learning and data science can be used to assess competencies of different nature across various contexts.

I am currently working as a postdoctoral researcher, applying large language models and statistical techniques to analyze free-text responses and structured survey data from health research projects such as the Swiss Multiple Sclerosis Registry.

Outside of work, I love cooking, baking, and travelling. I enjoy cross-stitching, reading, and occasionally going for a hike. I am also into puzzles and board games, and on top of that, I collect ducks.
