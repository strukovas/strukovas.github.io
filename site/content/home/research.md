- title: 'SpHeRe: exploring health narratives'
  label: Main project
  description: I developed SpHeRe, a browser-based system for linked exploratory analysis of health-related free
    text. It brings sentiment and emotion analysis, topic modelling and PII anonymisation into one workflow, helping
    researchers explore patient narratives and interview transcripts through interactive visualisations and downloadable
    reports. SpHeRe supports English, German, French and Italian.
- title: Patient narratives and future events
  description: I turn patient-authored narratives into structured, ordered life events and develop research models
    to predict future events in patients' disease journeys. This work combines language technologies with sequence
    modelling to explore how experiences unfold over time.
- title: Patient similarity and virtual twins
  description: I investigate how patient narratives and structured registry data can be combined to identify people
    with similar experiences and disease characteristics, and examine how these similarities relate to health-related
    quality of life.

My current work at the University of Zurich centres on building research tools with practical impact.\
I combine applied data analysis, statistics and machine learning to make complex data useful and connect technical methods with questions that matter for health research and people's lives.
